The TexaWave ERP Finance module is built on an enterprise-grade double-entry general ledger architecture. It governs organizational finances across Expenses, Customer Payments Received, Vendor Payments Made, Bank Accounts & Reconciliation, Chart of Accounts, General Ledger posting, and GST Tax Compliance. 1. Finance Module Architecture System Blueprint

1.1. High-Level Architectural Pattern

The TexaWave ERP Finance module is engineered as a domain-driven, layered enterprise application adhering to clean architecture principles. It separates concerns across a modern web presentation tier, a RESTful API gateway, domain business logic services, transactional repositories, and a multi-schema PostgreSQL database.

1.2. Application Tiers & Request Lifecycle

1. Presentation Tier (Next.js 14 App Router)

The frontend utilizes Next.js 14 with React Server Components and Client Components. Navigation is strictly decoupled:

  • Finance Workspace: /finance (Landing page), /finance/expenses, /finance/payments-received, /finance/payments-made, /finance/banking, /finance/accounting (General Ledger default), and /finance/reports.
  • Employee Portal: /employee-portal/expense-claims for draft submission and status tracking.
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS with TexaOne Prime Blue (#1769F5, #0A3271), Cyan (#12CFE3), and Nunito Sans typography. Zero violet/purple shades.
2. API Gateway & Validation Tier (NestJS 10)

The backend API runs on NestJS 10 with modular controllers:

  • Authentication & Context: JwtAuthGuard validates bearer tokens and binds the tenant context to request.user.organizationId.
  • DTO Validation: Global ValidationPipe with class-validator enforces types, non-empty fields, date ranges, and bounded pagination (@Max(100)) to prevent buffer overflows.
  • Controllers: 13 controllers handle routing, query sanitization, and HTTP response formatting.
3. Domain Services & Business Logic Tier

Encapsulates financial invariants, tax arithmetic, and transaction lifecycles:

  • ExpensesService: Category normalization via canonical mapping, claim auto-provisioning, GST line computation.
  • PaymentsReceivedService: Bank inflow coordination, invoice allocation balance checking.
  • PaymentsMadeService: Vendor disbursement validation, bill settlement logic.
  • BankingService: Balance reconciliation, CSV bank feed parser, running balance computation.
  • FinancialReportsService: Dynamic real-time aggregation of Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, and P&L.
4. Transactional Repository & Database Tier

Ensures ACID compliance and double-entry mathematical parity:

  • Atomic Units of Work: Repositories use prisma.$transaction to execute multi-table mutations atomically (e.g., updating bank account balance, inserting a bank transaction, and posting balanced journal lines).
  • Multi-Schema PostgreSQL: Financial tables reside in dedicated schema "finance" with physical foreign keys to "platform", "crm", and "scm".

1.3. Multi-Tenant Isolation & Security Architecture

Multi-tenancy is enforced at the database level. Every entity in schema "finance" includes an organization_id Int column referencing platform.organizations(id) with ON DELETE RESTRICT ON UPDATE CASCADE. Tenant context is injected automatically into every database query, ensuring that no tenant can read or mutate financial records belonging to another tenant.

3.2. Customer Payments Received & Bank-Inflow Workflow

Customer receipts execute an atomic multi-table unit-of-work via PaymentsReceivedRepository.postAndConfirmReceipt(). This ensures that bank balances, audit transactions, and general ledger accounts update concurrently without data drift.

Accounting Entries (Balanced JV-YYYYMM-XXXX)
  • Debit: Asset Account 1020 (Bank Account) = +₹9,000.00
  • Credit: Asset Account 1200 (Accounts Receivable) = -₹9,000.00
  • Net Trial Balance Impact: ₹0.00 (Perfect equilibrium)
Voiding & Reversal Protocol
  • Voiding triggers postAndVoidReceipt() within a transaction.
  • Generates an offsetting DEBIT bank transaction (-₹9,000.00).
  • Posts a reversal JV swapping Debit 1200 AR and Credit 1020 Bank.

3.3. General Ledger Posting & Immutability Rules

The General Ledger is the immutable single source of truth. Manual Journal Vouchers undergo strict structural validation before being committed. Once posted, vouchers cannot be altered or deleted.

3.4. GST & Tax Integration Processing

GSTR-1 Outward Supplies Engine

Aggregates outward taxable sales:

  • B2B Invoices (Table 4A): Intra-state (CGST + SGST) and inter-state (IGST) breakdowns against registered customer GSTINs.
  • B2C Large & Small (Table 5 & 7): Unregistered consumer transactions grouped by POS (Place of Supply).
  • HSN/SAC Summary (Table 12): 4-digit and 6-digit tariff code aggregation with total quantity, taxable value, and tax amounts.
GSTR-3B & Input Tax Credit (ITC)

Calculates monthly net cash tax liabilities:

  • Eligible ITC (Table 4A): Input tax credit accumulated from verified vendor bills and business expense tax lines.
  • Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): Tax liability on unregistered vendor services accounted under self-invoicing rules.
  • Net Tax Payable: Automatically nets output liability against available eligible ITC before cash ledger utilization.

5. Financial Projections — New Feature Concept & Design PROPOSED NEW FEATURE

CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL BOUNDARY: PROPOSED DESIGN ONLY
This section specifies a proposed future expansion module for TexaWave ERP. It is NOT an implemented part of the current application code or database schema. Forecast calculations are strictly read-only simulations and MUST NEVER mutate actual bank balances, create operational payments, or post journal lines to the General Ledger.

5.1. Business Purpose & Problems Solved

Growing enterprises require forward-looking financial visibility to make informed hiring, capital expenditure, and procurement decisions. The proposed Financial Projections engine solves critical operational vulnerabilities:

  • Eliminating Cash Surprises: Identifies cash deficits weeks before they materialize, allowing management to draw on credit lines or accelerate collections.
  • Dynamic Runway Estimation: Calculates operational runway under varying revenue conditions without relying on static spreadsheets.
  • Scenario Stress-Testing: Simulates the impact of macro shocks (customer payment delays, inflation on supplier bills).

5.2. Projections Architecture & Ingestion Flow

5.3. Projection Horizons & Multi-Scenario Simulation

The engine computes forward projections across three standard rolling horizons: 6-Month, 9-Month, and 12-Month. Each horizon models three distinct probabilistic operating scenarios:

Scenario Customer AR Inflow Weight (P) DSO Delay Factor Committed AP Outflow Unplanned OpEx Buffer Business Interpretation
Best-Case 95% of outstanding invoices -5 days (Early collection discount) 100% (Early settlement discounts captured) +0% contingency Optimal collection efficiency, minimal defaults, favorable vendor terms.
Expected-Case 85% of outstanding invoices 0 days (Standard contract term) 100% scheduled bills +5% contingency buffer Normal operating velocity based on 12-month historical collection trends.
Worst-Case 65% of outstanding invoices +30 days delayed collection 105% (Late penalties, emergency costs) +20% contingency buffer Macro stress scenario; major client default, delayed pipeline, elevated costs.

5.4. Mathematical Formulation & Proposed Calculations

For any monthly forecast period $t in [1, N]$ where $N in {6, 9, 12}$:

$$ ext{Projected Closing Cash}_t = ext{Opening Cash}_t + ext{Inflows}_t - ext{Outflows}_t$$
Where:
• $ ext{Opening Cash}_1 = sum ext{finance.bank_accounts.current_balance}$ (Active tenant bank accounts)
• $ ext{Opening Cash}_t = ext{Projected Closing Cash}_{t-1}$ (for $t > 1$)
• $ ext{Inflows}_t = sum_{ ext{invoices}} ( ext{Outstanding AR} imes P_{ ext{collect}}) + ext{Recurring MRR}_t$
• $ ext{Outflows}_t = sum_{ ext{bills}} ( ext{Committed AP}) + ext{Payroll Burn} + ext{Fixed OpEx} + ext{Tax Reserve}$

5.7. Permissions, Financial Safety & Implementation Roadmap

Financial Safety & Non-Modifying Invariant
The Projections engine is strictly an analytical query layer. It possesses READ-ONLY access to transactional tables (bank_accounts, journal_lines, expenses, payments_received). Under no circumstances can a forecasting job invoke posting methods on JournalPostingService or alter current_balance. Access requires the explicit permission finance:projections_view or finance:projections_manage.

6. Technical References and Glossary Developer Directory

6.1. Financial Terminology Glossary

Term Formal Definition Operational Application in TexaWave ERP
Double-Entry Accounting System where every transaction records equal and opposite debits and credits ($sum D = sum C$). Enforced by JournalPostingService; rejected if debit sum does not match credit sum.
Chart of Accounts (CoA) Hierarchical index of all financial accounts grouped by type (Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, Expense). Governed by FinanceAccount; structured with 4-digit nominal codes (1000s to 5000s).
Accounts Receivable (AR) Money owed to the enterprise by customers for delivered goods or services. Recognized under account 1200; credited upon confirmation of FinancePaymentReceived.
Accounts Payable (AP) Money owed by the enterprise to suppliers and vendors for delivered bills. Recognized under account 2000; debited upon confirmation of FinancePaymentMade.
Input Tax Credit (ITC) GST paid on inward business purchases that can be offset against output tax liability. Recorded in FinanceGstEntry and tracked via verified expense GST line items.
Reverse Charge (RCM) Liability where the recipient of goods/services is liable to pay tax instead of the supplier. Supported in GST reporting for unregistered vendor invoices.
Reconciled Balance Bank statement balance verified to match internal GL bank asset ledger. Tracked in FinanceBankTransaction.is_reconciled linking 1:1 to journal voucher.
