3. Database Architecture & ER Review

The Finance database consists of 11 models located in packages/database/prisma/schema.prisma within the finance schema. Below are the architectural structural diagrams and relationship models.

3.1 Subsystem Entity-Relationship Overview

PostgreSQL Schema: @@schema("finance") Entity Linkages
• FinanceExpense: Scoped to Organization; links to FinanceMasterListItem (category & mode), FinanceBankAccount (source), FinanceAccount (GL expense 5xxx), FinanceJournalEntry (voucher), and FinanceGstEntry.
• FinanceAccount: Self-referential tree via parentAccountId; links to FinanceJournalLine (ledger lines) and FinanceBankAccount.
• FinanceJournalEntry & Lines: Master-detail voucher relationship (onDelete: Cascade); reversal symmetry via reversedEntryId.
• FinanceBankAccount & Transactions: Bank account containment (onDelete: Cascade); matched to FinanceJournalEntry.
• FinancePaymentReceived & Allocations: Receipt master with invoice allocations (onDelete: Cascade); linked to crm.Account.
• FinancePaymentMade: Vendor disbursement linked to scm.ScmSupplierPayment, bank, and GL.
• FinanceGstEntry: Central tax register tracking outward and inward supplies with polymorphic linkage (sourceType, sourceId).
